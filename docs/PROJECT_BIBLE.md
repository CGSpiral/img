## A. Owner directives (from the project brief, 2026-10-27) ✅ D-001…D-003
- A complete, polished Roblox anime battlegrounds game inspired by the atmosphere and combat
  fantasy of Solo Leveling, not a demo.
- The owner is creative director and approves every major creative decision.
- An original game identity that uses only material we have permission to use.
- Server-authoritative, exploit-resistant multiplayer. PC, mobile and controller support.
- A custom map built from the owner's references.
- Work is resumable across sessions through the project documents.

## B. Vision statement 🟡
> Rise from the weakest hunter to a wielder of shadow in fast, cinematic battles fought across
> a city torn open by gates.

## C. Pillars 🟡
1. **Every hit feels powerful and reads clearly.** Hitstop, sound and camera are tuned with
   each move, never added afterwards.
2. **Shadow-and-gate fantasy in an original world.** Inspired by the genre, owned by us.
3. **Fair and secure.** Skill decides fights. The server decides outcomes.
4. **Plays well everywhere.** Touch and controller are first-class, with mobile-safe effects.

## D. Identity 🟡 (DISCOVERY A1–A5)
- Public title: not chosen. Options: GATEFALL, Umbral Ascent, Rankbreaker.
- Tone: proposed as a dark world with explosive combat.
- Art direction: options 1 Neon Abyss, 2 Ashen Monochrome, 3 Fractured City.

## E. Combat 🟡 (DISCOVERY C1–C6)
Proposed: fast and aggressive pace, soft lock-on, lag compensation capped at 200 ms, no gore.

## K. IP and content policy ✅ D-002
- No protected names, characters, logos, music, artwork or distinctive designs from Solo
  Leveling (novel, webtoon or anime) in shipped content. That includes, for example,
  character names, the franchise title, its logo and its soundtrack.
- Genre conventions are fine to use with original expression: hunters, gates and dungeons,
  rank tiers, shadow magic, summoned soldiers, a game-like "system" interface.
- Reference images supplied for mood are never shipped unless the owner documents
  permission in ASSET_REGISTER.md.
- Before public release we run an IP clean pass (ROADMAP M10). This is practical risk
  management, not legal advice. For a monetized release, consider a review by a lawyer.
